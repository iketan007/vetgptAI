# Vaccinations & Preventive Care

## Core puppy/kitten vaccines
Puppies typically receive a DHPP (distemper, hepatitis, parainfluenza,
parvovirus) series starting around 6-8 weeks, boosted every 3-4 weeks
until about 16 weeks, plus rabies as required by local law. Kittens
follow a similar schedule for FVRCP (feline viral rhinotracheitis,
calicivirus, panleukopenia) and rabies.

## Adult boosters
Most core vaccines are boosted annually or every 3 years depending on
the vaccine type and local regulations. Lifestyle vaccines (e.g. Bordetella
for dogs that go to daycare/boarding, feline leukemia for outdoor cats)
depend on individual risk factors — a vet can help decide which apply.

## Parasite prevention
Monthly flea/tick and heartworm (dogs) preventives are recommended
year-round in most climates. Annual fecal exams check for intestinal
parasites. Heartworm testing is usually required before starting
prevention in dogs.

## Spay/neuter
Spaying/neutering is commonly done between 4-9 months of age and can
reduce risk of certain cancers and unwanted litters. Timing can vary by
breed size, so discuss with a vet.
