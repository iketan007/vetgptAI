# Grooming & Hygiene

## Coat care
Short-haired pets usually need brushing weekly; long-haired or
double-coated breeds often need brushing several times a week to
prevent matting and reduce shedding. Bathing frequency depends on
coat type and lifestyle — most dogs do fine every 4-6 weeks, cats
rarely need baths at all.

## Nails
Overgrown nails can affect gait and cause pain. Most dogs and cats
need a nail trim every 3-4 weeks. If you can hear nails clicking on
hard floors, they're likely due for a trim.

## Ears
Check ears weekly for odor, redness, or excessive wax — these can
signal infection, especially in floppy-eared dog breeds. Clean only
with a vet-approved ear cleanser; avoid cotton swabs deep in the canal.

## Dental care
Daily tooth brushing with pet-safe toothpaste is the gold standard for
preventing periodontal disease. Dental chews and water additives help
but don't replace brushing. Bad breath, drooling, or reluctance to eat
can indicate dental pain and warrant a vet check.
